Aspects/Domains Covered: Deploy, configure, and maintain systems (Schedule tasks using at, cron, and systemd timer units)

Desc

[Unit]
Desc

Unit=system-check.service

[Install]

systemctl enable --now system-check.timer

systemctl list-timers
systemctl status system-check.timer
journalctl -u system-check.service
tail -n 5 /var/log/system-check.log
